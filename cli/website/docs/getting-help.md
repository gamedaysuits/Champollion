# Getting help

**Having trouble, or want help with your project? Let's talk.**
Write to [info@champollion.dev](mailto:info@champollion.dev?subject=Help%20with%20a%20language%20project).

You never need us to build a translator: the rails are made so that you, or
an AI assistant you trust, can go all the way on your own. But some projects
go faster, or feel safer, with people beside you.

## What help can look like

Whatever your project needs, worked out with you:

- **Getting set up** — on your computers, the way your community wants.
- **Organising your data** — bringing scattered files together, choosing a
  spelling system, setting aside good test sentences.
- **Teaching and training** — showing your team how the rails work, so you
  can carry on without us.
- **Building with you** — doing some of the technical work, under your
  direction.
- **Running things for you** — if your community asks, and only if it
  chooses to.

There's no price list: we work out what makes sense with each community, for
what it needs.

## Who we are

Champollion is a non-Indigenous project. We work in a service role, and only
by consent: your community decides what happens to its language, its data and
anything built from it. We take no share of anything a community earns from
its own language.

## Other ways to get help

- **[Join a project](/docs/join-a-project)** — list your project so other
  people can offer sentences, word lists or methods.
- **Your AI assistant** — it can explain any step. Ask it *"why does this step
  matter?"* and it will tell you what the rails say.
