# Join a project

A translator for a language with few written sentences needs many hands.
**Join a project** is how they find each other: people building a translator
list their project, say what would help, and others offer it.

## If you want to help

Browse the projects on the [Join a project](/join) page. Each one shows the
two languages, what the project is for, and what its holders are asking for —
sentences on certain topics, a word list, a method to try.

When you offer something:

- **You send it to the project, not to the public.** Your file is uploaded to
  Champollion's project storage, where only you, that project's holders, and
  the people who run that storage can open it.
- **It's held by you both.** You submitted it; they decide whether to take
  it. When they accept it, a note records who offered it, who accepted it,
  and what was agreed.
- **They see who you are.** The project's holders see the name and email you
  offered under, so they can reach you. The public page never shows an email.
- **They can say no.** A project may decline an offer.
- **You can withdraw it** before they've accepted it. Withdrawing (or their
  declining) changes the offer's status but doesn't delete the file, and the
  project's holders can still open it — so offer only what you're content for
  them to keep.
- **Its note comes first.** The tools won't offer a file marked private, or
  one whose speakers haven't all agreed — only its holders can change that.

What you won't get from a project: its sentences. Nothing of theirs is sent
to you without their yes.

## If you're building a translator

You can list your project once it's started on the rails. You'll say:

- the two languages, and what the translator is for,
- the name you want shown — never your email,
- what would help most.

Every listing gets a short review before it appears, so the page stays
trustworthy. When someone offers something, you decide: accept it, and it
arrives in your project's inbox with a note naming both of you, ready to bring
in like any file — or decline it.

## What can be offered today

Files: sentences, word lists, and ways of translating to try. Helping in other ways —
checking drafts from afar, or lending computer power — is not part of it yet.

When no projects are listed yet, the page says so honestly.
